{% trans "How to write a reference" %}

{% trans "A reference shares your experience. It is not a verdict on another person." %}

  • {% trans "Describe what happened." %} {% trans "What did you see, hear and do together? Stay concrete, the way a camera would have recorded it." %}
  • {% trans "Speak from yourself." %} {% trans "Write “I felt …” or “I was glad that …” rather than “they are …”." %}
  • {% trans "Name your feelings and needs." %} {% trans "“I felt uneasy because I need clear agreements” tells others more than “unreliable”." %}
  • {% trans "Leave out labels and judgements." %} {% trans "Words like rude, lazy, toxic or perfect describe your judgement, not the person. Let your experience speak." %}
  • {% trans "Take responsibility for your feelings." %} {% trans "What others do can touch your feelings; the feelings come from your own needs. Say what you would have wished for." %}
  • {% trans "Write as if they were reading along." %} {% trans "They will. Be honest and kind at the same time." %}
